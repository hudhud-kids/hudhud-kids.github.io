# Hudhud v2 — scope and sequence (20 levels × 4 units)

Format: unit topic | language function (the unit's ONE function) | grammar focus. Lesson 6 of each unit is the big task.
CEFR per level is fixed. Typical ages: L1–4 6–9 y, L5–8 8–12 y, L9–12 11–15 y, L13–20 13–18 y.
Grammar that appears in a level must not be "taught" again as new in a later level (only recycled); later levels build on it.

## L1 — Pre-A1 (Starters) · GSE 10-15
1. Hello and Me | greeting, introducing yourself | I'm … / My name is … / be (I, you)
2. My Family | saying who people are | This is my … / He's / She's …
3. Colours and Toys | naming and describing things | It's a red ball. adjective + noun, a/an
4. Numbers and Age | counting, saying age, how many | How old are you? I'm 7. / How many …? numbers 1–20

## L2 — Pre-A1 (Starters) · GSE 16-21
1. My Body and Clothes | describing appearance | I've got … / plurals / his, her
2. Food I Like | likes and dislikes | I like / I don't like / Do you like …? Yes, I do.
3. Animals | saying what animals can do | can / can't
4. My Room and School Things | saying where things are | in / on / under / next to; Where is …?

## L3 — A1 (Movers) · GSE 22-25
1. My Day | describing routines | present simple (I/you/we), telling the time
2. My Town | describing places | there is / there are; Is there …?
3. At the Shop | asking for things and prices | Can I have …? How much is/are …?
4. Weather and Seasons | describing weather and what people wear | It's sunny; present simple he/she (-s)

## L4 — A1 (Movers) · GSE 26-29
1. Right Now | saying what is happening | present continuous
2. Free Time | asking how often | How often …? always/usually/sometimes/never
3. Finding the Way | giving simple directions | imperatives; prepositions of place
4. Helping at Home | asking for and offering help | Can you …? Could you …? Let me …

## L5 — A2 (Flyers) · GSE 30-32
1. Last Weekend | telling what happened | past simple (regular), time words
2. Trips and Visits | asking about past trips | past simple irregular, Did you …? Where did …?
3. Bigger, Better, Best | comparing | comparatives and superlatives
4. Food and Health | giving simple advice | should / shouldn't

## L6 — A2 (Flyers / Key) · GSE 33-35
1. Weekend Plans | talking about future plans | be going to; future time words
2. Invitations | inviting, accepting, declining politely | Would you like to …? I'd love to. / I'm afraid I can't …
3. How I Feel | describing feelings and reasons | -ed / -ing adjectives; because / so
4. Rules | talking about rules and obligations | have to / don't have to / must / mustn't

## L7 — A2+ (Key for Schools) · GSE 36-42
1. Have You Ever …? | talking about experiences | present perfect with ever / never / already / yet
2. Choices | expressing preferences | prefer … to …, would rather, I'd prefer to
3. What Happened? | telling a story about the past | past continuous vs past simple, when / while
4. The Future | predicting and promising | will / won't / might, I promise I'll …

## L8 — B1 (Preliminary) · GSE 43-46
1. In My Opinion | giving and asking for opinions | I think / In my opinion / What do you think about …?
2. I Agree … or Not | agreeing and disagreeing politely | So do I / Neither do I / I see your point, but …
3. What Should I Do? | asking for and giving advice | If I were you …, Why don't you …?, first conditional
4. People and Places in Detail | describing in detail | relative clauses who / which / where / whose

## L9 — B1 (Preliminary) · GSE 47-50
1. Just Checking | checking and confirming understanding | question tags; Do you mean …? So you're saying …
2. The News | reporting what others said | reported speech (said / told / asked)
3. Would You Mind? | making polite requests and asking permission | Would you mind …ing? Is it OK if …?
4. Making Decisions | comparing options and deciding | It would be better to …, Let's go for …, as … as

## L10 — B1+ · GSE 51-54
1. Experiences in Detail | describing experiences and how long | present perfect continuous, for / since
2. The Facts | giving precise information | passive voice (present and past)
3. There's a Problem | complaining politely and asking for solutions | I'm afraid …, Could you possibly …? I'd like to …
4. Imagine That | imagining unreal situations | second conditional, I wish + past

## L11 — B1+ · GSE 55-58
1. One Hundred Percent Sure | expressing certainty and possibility | must / might / can't be; I'm positive that …
2. You Should Try It | persuading and recommending | It's worth …ing, You really should …, If you ask me …
3. Looking Back | talking about regrets and past possibilities | should have / could have, I wish I had …
4. How It Works | explaining processes and instructions | sequencing words, passive in processes

## L12 — B2 (First) · GSE 59-62
1. Picture This | describing and speculating about pictures | It looks as if …, might have / must have done
2. Pros and Cons | balancing arguments | On the one hand …, whereas, although, despite
3. Let's Make a Deal | negotiating and compromising | How about if …? provided that, as long as
4. Tell Me a Story | narrating with impact | past perfect, narrative tenses, suddenly / eventually

## L13 — B2 (First) · GSE 63-66
1. Taking a Stand | stating and defending a position | I'd argue that …, That may be true, but …
2. What If It Had …? | discussing consequences of past events | third and mixed conditionals
3. Presenting Ideas | giving a short structured talk | signposting: Firstly, Moving on to …, To sum up
4. Speaking Together | collaborative decision tasks (B2 First Part 3–4) | suggesting, evaluating, reaching agreement

## L14 — B2+ · GSE 67-71
1. Saying It Tactfully | hedging and softening | It seems that …, tends to, I'm not entirely sure that …
2. Joining the Discussion | managing turn-taking | Sorry to interrupt, Can I just add …, Going back to …
3. What I Really Mean | emphasising | cleft sentences (What I mean is …, It was … that …)
4. Judging Ideas | evaluating ideas and solutions | It's debatable whether …, The main drawback is …

## L15 — B2+ · GSE 72-75
1. The Evidence Says | supporting claims with evidence | According to …, Research suggests …, reporting verbs
2. Formal or Friendly? | adapting register to the situation | formal vs informal expressions
3. Solving It Together | proposing and evaluating solutions in a meeting | It might be worth …, What if we …?
4. The Long Turn | IELTS Part 2–3 style long answers | extending answers, examples, reasons, contrasts

## L16 — C1 (Advanced) · GSE 76-80
1. Making It Powerful | persuading with emphasis | inversion (Never have I …, Not only …), rhetorical questions
2. Can We Trust It? | evaluating sources and claims | modal perfects for deduction, It's highly unlikely that …
3. The Big Talk | structuring an extended talk | discourse markers, summarising, rhetorical structure
4. C1 Advanced Speaking | long turn and discussion at C1 | comparing, speculating, justifying

## L17 — C1 (Advanced) · GSE 81-84
1. Yes, But … | conceding and qualifying arguments | Admittedly …, That said …, Granted, …
2. In Other Words | explaining complex ideas and paraphrasing | What this means is …, To put it simply …
3. Causes and Effects | discussing global issues | This has led to …, stem from, give rise to
4. IELTS 7+ | high-band fluent discussion | idiomatic language, abstract topics

## L18 — C2 (Proficiency) · GSE 85-87
1. Precise Arguments | arguing with precision in academic debate | nominalisation, precise reporting verbs
2. Were That to Happen | speculating about the future | advanced conditionals (Were … to, Should …), future perfect
3. Who We Are | discussing identity and culture | idiomatic and figurative language
4. Academic Presentations | presenting and handling questions | fielding questions, signposting at C2

## L19 — C2 (Proficiency) · GSE 88-89
1. The Art of Persuasion | using rhetoric | tricolon, contrast, rhetorical devices
2. Debate Rounds | building cases and rebuttal | motion, burden of proof, rebuttal phrases
3. Hard Questions | discussing ethics and society | weighing values, conditional concession
4. Formal Speeches | formal presentations and ceremonies | formal register, quotation, closing

## L20 — C2 (Mastery) · GSE 90
1. Between the Lines | expressing and catching implicit meaning | irony, understatement, implication
2. Leading the Seminar | leading academic discussion | inviting, summarising, synthesising views
3. The Public Debate | public debate and persuasion | handling hostile questions, reframing
4. Band 9 | CPE / IELTS 9 mastery | precision, range, natural idiom
