# Notes for writers of the remaining levels (from the review of levels 4, 5, 8–13)

Already published v2 levels (read their unit list and a few scripts to stay consistent): content/v2/L4, L5, L8, L9, L10, L11, L12, L13.

1. Kit dialogues (`kits[key].dialogue`) must be a SHORTER version of the model dialogue: 6–8 lines, not a copy of the whole thing.
2. Every `focus` sentence must appear word for word in p1 or p2, start with a capital letter and be a full sentence.
3. Try to use all 6 expressions of the lesson in the model dialogue.
4. Do not present as new any grammar that a lower level already teaches (see SCOPE.md); recycle it instead. Do not use grammar that a higher level teaches as its main focus.
5. Topics already used a lot: screen time, Riyadh Season, AlUla, Abha. Prefer other topics.
6. Avoid gambling-flavoured idioms (odds are, safe bet, long shot, hold all the aces).
7. If a dialogue tells about a match, an event or news that is invented, make that clear (e.g. "in our school league").
8. Answer keys: check that the marked answer is the ONLY correct option — reviewers found many items where a distractor was also acceptable (e.g. "speed/pace", "say/tell", "view/opinion").
9. Facts in culture points must be accurate; when unsure, write a general true statement rather than a precise number.
